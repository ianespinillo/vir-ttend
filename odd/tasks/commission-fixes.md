# Workshop commission fixes
Objective: scope subject rosters to the subject's workshop commission, allow 1 or 2 commissions per course, and model 7th year (technical) as workshop-only attendance.
Branch: `fix/commission-subject-students` (from `dev`) · Delivery: ask-on-risk · Test runner: jest (`cd apps/api && npx jest <path>`), vitest (`@repo/ui`).

## Specs
- S1. Bug: "le asigne a un profesor una materia de una comision especifica y le trae todos los alumnos, no los propios de la comision". A workshop subject's roster (GET /subjects/:id/students, GET /attendance/subject) only lists active students of the commission(s) its schedule slots target. Commission comes from slot `commissionNumber` (null = both).
- S2. Mixed slots ("si, puede ser que haya horarios mixtos"): when a subject has slots for different commissions, the roster for a date uses the slots of that date's weekday (commission 1 on Monday → commission 1 students). If the weekday has no slot, or any slot that day targets both, the roster is every commission the subject targets. GET /subjects/:id/students without a date returns the union.
- S3. If the course has no commissions, or a slot targets both, the roster is the whole active course (today's behavior).
- S4. Writes follow the roster: "mark all" (POST /attendance/subject/all) only affects the subject's roster for that date and only ACTIVE students (today it also includes inactive ones — found during exploration); registering subject attendance rejects students outside the roster.
- S5. "un curso puede tener comisiones (puede que no y sea solo 1, lo que sigue implicando un preceptor de taller)": a course can have 1 or 2 workshop commissions; each has its workshop preceptor. With 1 commission every active student belongs to it.
- S6. "en la asignacion de materias hay que mostrar condicionalmente la seleccion de comision si es una sola, considero que es innecesario el select": the commission select of the schedule slot form is hidden when the course has a single commission (slot saved with `commissionNumber` null).
- S7. "lo unico que no tiene 7mo son materias de teoria, es decir, la asistencia diaria de 7mo es solo de taller (por lo que deberia tomarse como 1pto entero)" + "la asistencia solo la toma el prece de taller ... y no existe la diaria en si": for 7th-year technical courses, daily (theory) attendance does not exist; attendance is the workshop attendance taken by the commission's workshop preceptor, and a workshop absence weighs 1 (instead of 0.5). Detection (decision, 2026-10-18): course `level === SECONDARY` and `yearNumber === 7` (primary also has a 7th grade). Late 0.25 / late beyond tolerance 0.5 unchanged (assumption).
